Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Lumanworth is a software development company in Perth, Western Australia. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can ask of us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What this website collects

Nothing on its own. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Two services see your visit: the fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is served by Vercel. Each receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the page, and Vercel keeps standard server logs for a short time for security. We do not see or use those logs to identify you.

What you send us

When you email us, we keep your name, your email address and what you wrote. If we go on to work together, we keep what the work needs: your contact details, the documents you share, and the records of the engagement.

How we use it

To reply, to do the work, and to keep the records a business must keep. We do not sell information and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the services we run the business on, such as email hosting and accounting, and when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that go nowhere are deleted within a year. Records of work we have done are kept for as long as Australian tax and company law requires, then deleted.

The products we build

Each app and system we build for a client belongs to that client and has its own privacy policy, published by them. This page covers lumanworth.com and the email you send us, nothing more.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to support@lumanworth.com and we will answer. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
